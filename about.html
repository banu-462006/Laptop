Our Story

Grown from a single farmers' market stall

TerraBloom began in 2019 as a Saturday market stand run by two friends with a compost bin and a folding table. Today we're still guided by the same rule: nothing goes on a plate that we wouldn't be proud to grow ourselves.

Since 2019

Slow food, served fast

We work directly with 18 farms within 50 miles of our kitchen, paying fair prices for produce picked the same week it's served. No freezers, no central commissary — just a kitchen that opens at 5am and closes when the last bowl ships.

Every recipe on our menu started as a seasonal special before earning a permanent spot, tested first on the people who know us best: our regulars.
